Search by meaning

Find a section by what you are trying to do, in English or Danish

Search the guide by what you are trying to do, in English or Danish. It knows synonyms and register terms: moves abroad finds emigration, uddannelse finds hfaudd.
Last modified

November 23, 2026

The guide has two searches. The search box in the top bar finds the exact words you type. The search on this page also looks for synonyms and for the English term behind a Danish word, and it takes you to the section, not just the page: moves abroad finds emigration, uddannelse finds hfaudd, and a pasted error message finds the table row that explains it.

It works from a hand-written list of synonyms, not from a language model. A word that is not on the list is matched as you typed it (small typing errors are corrected). If a search that should have worked comes up empty, write it in the feedback box on this page so the word can be added.

Looking for a column rather than an explanation? Find a variable searches every column in every register.
